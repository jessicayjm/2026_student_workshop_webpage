This year's annual TTIC Student Workshop will include student talks, an invited talk, and a panel discussion.

Each student talk will be 20 (short talk) or 30 (long talk) minutes, including 5 minutes for questions. We will present a Best Talk Award.

We are glad to have Bowen Shi as our invited alumnus, and he will talk about his work on a series of audio foundation models.
